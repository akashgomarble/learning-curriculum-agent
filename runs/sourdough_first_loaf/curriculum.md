# Curriculum: Bake my first sourdough loaf this weekend; my starter is already active

**Budget:** 120 min · **Planned:** 101 min · **Videos:** 5 · **Candidates assessed:** 36

**How I read the goal:** The learner bakes a decent first sourdough boule this weekend with their active starter, understanding each step (levain, hydration, bulk, shaping, cold proof, scoring, Dutch oven bake) from at most two consistent bakers.

**Assumptions:** The 'weekend' bake means mixing Friday/Saturday, a cold retard overnight, and baking the next day, so a 1-2 day timeline is acceptable.; The learner owns or can get a Dutch oven, a kitchen scale, and a banneton or a bowl with a towel.; The learner wants a standard country-style white or lightly whole-wheat boule at around 70-75% hydration, not an advanced high-hydration loaf.; Since 'at most two bakers' methods' is required, the plan favors one primary full bake-along baker plus one optional secondary for troubleshooting.
> ⚠️ The near-duplicate cluster (4gEoh3sk2AE, CTuGXdyrWUo, QWiouzzCUcs) was not used because of the two-baker limit, so none of its members is kept.
> ⚠️ The plan uses 100.6 of 120 minutes (about 84%), leaving 19.4 minutes unused. The remaining good candidates are from other creators or duplicate the recipe, and adding them would break the two-baker limit.
> ⚠️ The baking is done by two bakers: The Bread Code (primary: the course, the hydration and fermentation concepts, shaping, and the cold-proof bake) and Keep it Sweet Kitchen (mistakes only). Follow the Bread Code recipe and use the Keep it Sweet video only as troubleshooting.
> ⚠️ Some segment maps do not match the listed durations (for example the course lists sections past its length). Whole videos are listed and counted at their stated minutes.
> ⚠️ The cold-proof video compares room-temperature and cold-retarded loaves; follow only the cold-retarded loaf.
> ⚠️ Assumes you have a Dutch oven, a scale and a banneton or a bowl with a towel, and a 1-2 day timeline with an overnight fridge proof.

## Plan

- **Hydration and building a levain from an active starter** (`levain_hydration`): They know yeasted dough but not baker's percentages or how to turn a starter into a levain that fits the weekend schedule.
- **Mixing, autolyse, and bulk fermentation with stretch-and-folds** (`mixing_bulk`): Yeast timing cues do not apply; they must learn to read dough (volume increase, bubbles, jiggle) instead of the clock, and replace kneading with folds.
- **Pre-shaping and shaping a boule with surface tension** (`shaping_boule`): Sandwich-loaf shaping is different; wetter sourdough needs tension-building technique to hold its shape.
- **Final proof and cold retard in the fridge** (`cold_retard`): Makes the weekend schedule workable and develops flavor; they need to know how to judge proofing with the poke test and avoid overproofing.
- **Scoring and baking in a preheated Dutch oven** (`scoring_dutch_oven`): Dutch oven steam and scoring control oven spring and crust, which they have never done with sandwich bread.
- **Follow a full beginner sourdough bake-along from start to finish** (`full_bakealong`): Ties all steps together into one consistent method so the learner can follow along at the counter.
- **Common beginner sourdough mistakes and fixes** (`beginner_mistakes`): Helps diagnose dense crumb, flat loaves, sticking dough, and underproofing/overproofing on a first bake.
