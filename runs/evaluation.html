Evaluation

Every test case is scored three ways: automatic checks in code (hard checks gate a pass), an AI grader from a different model family than the agent, and head-to-head comparisons in both orders. The agent is measured against two baselines on the same candidate pools — the same agent denied transcripts (does reading the content actually matter?), and the top videos by view count (is popularity enough?).

AI grader vs human labels

Every curriculum was also labelled pass/fail by a human from a blind sheet (32 labels). Grader and human agree on 91% of them (Cohen's κ 0.80, 95% CI 0.55–1.00; TPR 90%, TNR 92%). Of the disagreements, the grader was too lenient on 1 and too strict on 2; the full disagreement table is in evals/results/RESULTS.md.
